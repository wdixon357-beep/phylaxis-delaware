Applicant Information

Annual Membership
$10.00 a year · Chapter dues

Chapter dues of $10.00 a year are payable to the Chapter Treasurer once your application is approved. Nothing is due today, and nothing is collected through this form.

Membership in the national Phylaxis Society is separate, is paid directly to the national Society, and is $50.00 a year. It is not required in order to join this Chapter.
